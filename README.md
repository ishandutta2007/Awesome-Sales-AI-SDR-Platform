# Awesome-Sales-AI-SDR-Platform

## Top Sales AI SDR Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Autonomous Outbound Agents, Personalized Outreach, Lead Research, Multi-Channel Sequencing & Meeting Booking*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Sales Development Representatives (AI SDRs)**. These systems research prospects, personalize emails/LinkedIn messages, run sequences, handle replies, and book meetings — augmenting or partially replacing traditional SDR work.



**Examples** include 11x.ai, Artisan AI, Regie.ai, AiSDR, Jason AI by Reply.io, Humantic AI, Persana AI, Landbase, Octave, and Agent Frank (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully autonomous, production-grade AI SDR platforms remain largely commercial. Open options focus on **agent frameworks, local sales agents, CrewAI/LangChain pipelines, and human-in-the-loop outreach tools**. This section lists the strongest available projects and is realistic about the current gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OpenCloser](https://github.com/issacops/opencloser-v2)**  

  Open-source, local-first AI sales platform — strategist, lead researcher, voice caller (SDR), coach, and manager agents running on the desktop with CRM features.



- **[FundzWatch AI SDR / event-driven agents](https://github.com/Fund-z/fundzwatch-ai-sdr)**  

  Open-source AI SDR agent that finds, scores, and drafts personalized outreach based on real-time business events (funding, leadership changes, etc.).



- **[ai-sdr (CrewAI multi-agent)](https://github.com/avcap/ai-sdr)**  

  Multi-agent AI SDR platform using CrewAI for prospecting, personalization, outreach, and campaign coordination with email sequence support.



- **[opensource-sales-ops](https://github.com/mukul-07/opensource-sales-ops)**  

  Local-first, human-in-the-loop AI SDR pipeline for Claude Code — scans prospects, qualifies, drafts outreach, and manages cadence (never auto-sends).



- **[SalesGPT and conversational sales agents](https://github.com/)**  

  Open LLM-based sales conversation agents that handle stages such as qualification, objection handling, and multi-channel dialogue.



- **[Composio / agent SDKs for sales](https://github.com/ComposioHQ)**  

  Open SDKs and integration layers that let developers build custom AI agents connected to CRM, email, and outreach tools.



- **[b2b-sdr-agent-template and similar templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Production-oriented open templates for building WhatsApp/email SDR bots with layered context, memory, and tool use.



- **[LangChain / CrewAI / AutoGen sales examples](https://github.com/)**  

  Community agents and notebooks that implement research → personalize → sequence pipelines on top of popular agent frameworks.



- **[Open email sequencing and personalization scripts](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted or script-based tools for generating personalized cold email at scale (pair with your own sending infrastructure).



- **[Local LLM sales copilots](https://github.com/)**  

  Projects that run research and drafting agents entirely on local or private models for data-sensitive teams.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building a human-in-the-loop pipeline with **CrewAI/LangChain** agents that research and draft, while humans approve every send.

- Using event-triggered open agents (funding, hiring, tech-stack changes) for timely, signal-based outreach.

- Running **OpenCloser**-style local agents when data residency and zero SaaS fees are non-negotiable.

- Combining open enrichment APIs + open drafting agents + your existing sequencer (Outreach, Salesloft, Instantly, etc.).

- Accepting that deliverability, data quality, multi-channel orchestration, and reply handling at scale still favor mature commercial AI SDR platforms.

- Starting small with open tools for research and copy, then graduating to commercial agents for full autonomy.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Research with open web/search + enrichment → draft with LLM agents (CrewAI, LangChain, or local models) → sequence via open or existing tools → keep humans in the approval loop. This works for controlled volume and high personalization. Commercial AI SDRs (11x, Artisan, AiSDR, Regie, etc.) still lead when you need end-to-end autonomy, built-in data, multi-channel execution, reply handling, and measurable meeting volume at scale.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- AI SDR tools send messages to real people. Poor targeting, inaccurate personalization, or aggressive volume can damage domain reputation, violate anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, etc.), and harm brand trust. Open-source agents that auto-send require extreme caution. Always maintain human oversight, respect opt-outs, and monitor deliverability. This list is not sales, legal, or compliance advice.
